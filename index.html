Card Scanner

Take a photo of a business card. The details are read on your phone and you add them to Contacts with one tap.

Tip: lay the card on a plain, dark surface in good light and fill most of the frame.

Reading card…

The first scan loads the text reader (about 7 MB). After that it works offline.

Almost done. On the contact screen that opened, tap Create New Contact, then Done.

Nothing opened? Tap “Add to Contacts” again, or use “More ways to save” below.
